Your Personal 90-Day Wealth Mission

Your plan is built around your current financial starting point and your personal goal.

DAYS 1–30

Stabilise

Build a clear picture of your money and establish a practical foundation.

  • Track your income and expenses
  • Identify unnecessary spending
  • Set a realistic financial target
  • Start one consistent money habit
DAYS 31–60

Strengthen

Strengthen your financial resilience and improve your money knowledge.

  • Build your savings habit
  • Review your progress
  • Learn the fundamentals of your chosen financial goal
  • Reduce avoidable financial pressure
DAYS 61–90

Build

Turn your new habits into a repeatable long-term financial system.

  • Review your 90-day progress
  • Strengthen your savings or investing routine
  • Set your next measurable financial target
  • Create a plan for the next 90 days
