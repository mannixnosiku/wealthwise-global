Your 90-Day Wealth Mission

Wealth is built through consistent decisions, not one giant move. Your first 90 days are divided into three practical phases.

DAYS 1–30

Stabilise

Understand your money and strengthen the foundation.

  • Track income and expenses
  • Create a realistic spending plan
  • Identify financial leaks
  • Set your first measurable target
DAYS 31–60

Strengthen

Improve resilience and reduce financial pressure.

  • Build emergency savings
  • Develop a debt strategy if needed
  • Improve financial knowledge
  • Explore additional income skills
DAYS 61–90

Build

Begin building toward longer-term financial goals.

  • Review your financial progress
  • Strengthen your savings habit
  • Learn investment fundamentals
  • Create your next 90-day target
